## What each band communicates (hypothesis)

| Band | Communication | Conversion pressure | Quality perception | Margin room | Ads economics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| €79–99 | Accessible Italian leather; comparable to mid Alore/Fiora | Higher CVR potential | Risk of “cheap Italian” if photos weak | Tight unless COGS low | More CPL headroom if margins thin |
| €100–129 | Upper specialist cluster (near Lemisa heroes) | Medium | Stronger quality signal | Better | Needs stronger creative + trust |
| €130–169 | Above most local specialists | Lower CVR | Clearer premium | Good if COGS allows | Higher AOV helps CAC |
| €170–219+ | Premium gap vs local Italian shops | Hardest | Needs brand/story proof | Best per unit | Requires high intent |

Wave 1 **does not** default to cheapest. Placeholders should span the band (e.g. €89 / €99 / €109 / €119 / €129) to learn relative preference **with prices visible**.

---

## Experiment rules for price

1. **Never hide price** on the validation page.
2. Do not change all product prices mid-flight without logging in EXPERIMENTS.md.
3. Price A/B is **secondary** in Wave 1 after product, creative, audience.
4. Waitlist `preferred_product` is the WTP proxy (weak vs purchase, useful vs email-only).
5. After UX revisit, consider multi-band or deposit tests if 2A learning is ambiguous.
