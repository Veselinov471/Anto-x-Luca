# Project Discovery — Anto x Luca

**Status:** Working document for market validation  
**Market:** Bulgaria  
**Offer (Wave 1):** Early-access waitlist (email + preferred product + consent)  
**Price band (Wave 1):** ~€79–€129 (match local Italian-leather competitors)  
**Brand (working):** Anto x Luca

---

## 1. Business objective

Determine whether there is **meaningful demand** in Bulgaria for premium/accessibly-premium women's handbags made in Italy, **before** investing heavily in inventory, branding, or a full e-commerce platform.

### What success means for this experiment

Success is **evidence**, not vanity traffic. After a controlled Meta/Instagram spend (€300–€700) and a live landing page, we should be able to answer:

1. Do Bulgarian women in target segments engage with the bags (views, clicks, scroll)?
2. Do they accept visible prices in the €79–€129 band enough to select a preferred product?
3. Will they submit email + product preference for early access?
4. Which product / creative / audience combinations outperform others?
5. Is cost per qualified lead (CPL) compatible with a path to profitable CAC given unit economics?

### Explicit non-goals (Wave 1)

- Maximizing revenue or order volume
- Building a full store (cart, checkout, accounts, OMS)
- Final brand identity investment
- Large inventory commitment

## 3. Target customer hypothesis

Bulgarian women are **not** one segment. Initial segments to consider:

| Segment | Why it matters | Wave 1 priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Women 25–34 | Fashion discovery, Instagram-native, career/lifestyle spend | Primary |
| Women 35–44 | Higher disposable income, quality over quantity | Primary |
| Women 45–54 | Classic style, trust-sensitive | Secondary |
| Urban / Sofia | Concentration of online fashion spend | Primary geo |
| Other major cities (Plovdiv, Varna, Burgas) | Scale beyond Sofia | Include in geo |
| Fashion-conscious / accessibly-premium | Alignment with positioning | Filter via creative |
| Existing online fashion buyers | Lower friction for DTC | Assumed majority of Meta traffic |

---

## 4. Constraints and assets

**Assets already in place (per founder):**

- Relationship with Italian bag manufacturer
- Shipping/logistics connections

**Constraints:**

- Minimize inventory and platform spend before validation
- Bulgarian-first UX; English later
- GDPR / EU privacy
- Dual EUR + BGN price display during euro transition period (legal review required)
- No fake reviews, scarcity, or unsupported luxury claims

---

## 5. Validation approach

```
Meta ad → Landing page → Product interaction → Waitlist (email + preference) → Learn
```

Stronger intent signals (deposit, purchase) are **deferred** until after UI/UX revisit and unit-economics clearance. Offer type **1A** and price band **2A** may change after design critique.

---

## 6. Learning principle

> Maximum learning per euro spent.

A simple landing page that reliably answers who wants which product at what price, under which message, and at what acquisition cost, is a successful MVP.
