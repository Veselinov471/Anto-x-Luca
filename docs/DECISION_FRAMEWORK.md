# Decision Framework — Anto x Luca Validation

## Principle

Decide on **evidence** (behavior + economics), not opinions or vanity metrics (likes, raw sessions alone).

Email signup ≠ purchase. Prefer **qualified waitlist** (valid email + consent + product preference) and **CPL vs break-even scenarios**.

---

## Signal categories

### Green (continue / prepare Phase 3)

Directional examples — interpret with sample size, margins, and CPL:

- Healthy ad CTR for prospecting cold traffic
- Strong product interaction (clicks / views)
- Waitlist CVR meaningful relative to spend
- CPL fits base/optimistic unit-economics scenarios
- Clear SKU winners (H6)
- Prices in band do not kill interest

### Yellow (iterate)

- Interest high but form drop-off high → trust/UX/offer
- Strong traffic, weak product clicks → creative/product mismatch
- One age/geo works only → narrow ICP or messaging
- CPL only works under optimistic Q → test deposit/purchase next
- Craftsmanship creative works, value creative fails (or reverse) → reposition

### Red (stop or major rethink)

- Ads cannot generate interest at acceptable CPC/CTR
- Near-zero product interaction despite traffic
- Consistent price rejection (no preference / rage bounces after price)
- CPL incompatible even with optimistic conversion assumptions
- Trust objections dominate qualitative replies

**Do not invent hard universal % thresholds** without margin math. Always attach sample size.

---

## Outcome options

| Outcome | When | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Continue** | Green across product + intent + economics | Phase 3 planning |
| **Iterate** | Yellow — one clear bottleneck | Fix one variable; new experiment log |
| **Reposition** | Product interest exists under different message | Change VP; hold product |
| **Reprice** | Demand exists, economics fail at 2A | Test other bands carefully |
| **Product change** | One SKU carries all interest | Rebuild assortment around winner |
| **Stop** | Red on interest or irreparable economics | Do not invest €10k–€50k |

---

## UI/UX revisit gate (before paid spend)

Re-open:

1. Offer 1A vs reservation/deposit/purchase  
2. Price band 2A vs multi-band/premium  

If critique shows waitlist will teach too little per euro, upgrade offer before scaling ads.
