# Competitor Analysis — Bulgarian Handbag Market

**Research date:** 2026-09-26  
**Method:** Public web research (homepages and product listings).  
**Rule:** Do not fabricate. Items marked **NEEDS MANUAL VALIDATION** were not fully verified end-to-end.

---

## 1. Market snapshot

The Bulgarian online market for **Italian-made women's leather handbags** is **crowded**. Multiple Bulgarian e-shops already lead with “Made in Italy” / Florence / Tuscany / natural leather. For a new brand, **Made in Italy alone is table stakes**, not a unique advantage.

Observed specialist price band for Italian leather bags is roughly **€40–€135**, with many SKUs clustered **€50–€100**. Designer / accessible-luxury international brands (and Bulgarian-founded BY FAR globally) sit much higher and are not direct price peers for Wave 1 (€79–€129).

## 5. Competitive implications for Anto x Luca

1. **Do not rely on “Made in Italy” as the only hook** — competitors already own that language.
2. Wave 1 prices **€79–€129** sit at the **upper half** of the specialist cluster (competitive but not “cheap”).
3. Differentiation hypotheses to test: photography quality, quieter premium UI, verifiable craftsmanship story, curated 3–5 heroes, transparent dual pricing + BG support, honest policies without fake scarcity.
4. Trust baseline expected by shoppers: clear returns, shipping time, phone/email, company identity.
5. COD is common locally; our Wave 1 waitlist avoids payment friction but also **under-measures** true WTP — plan a later purchase/deposit test.
