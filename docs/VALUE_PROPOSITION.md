# Value Proposition Hypotheses — Anto x Luca

> Do **not** pick a final positioning prematurely. Wave 1 uses one on-page default and rotates ad creatives that stress different angles.

---

## Default on-page positioning (Wave 1)

**Craftsmanship + everyday elegance**

Working headline directions (Bulgarian — refine with native speaker):

- „Италиански чанти, създадени за всеки ден.“
- „Италианска изработка. Ежедневна елегантност.“
- „Малка селекция дамски чанти от Италия.“

- „Естествена кожа, изработена в Италия — без излишен шум, с внимание към детайла.“

CTA (matches offer 1A):

- „Получавам ранен достъп“

---

## Positioning A — Italian craftsmanship

**Promise:** Made in Italy, crafted for everyday elegance.

**Tests:** Does origin + craftsmanship language increase CTR and waitlist rate vs lifestyle-only?

**Risk:** Commodity claim — competitors already say this.

**Evidence needed:** Creative A vs C performance; FAQ opens on “Where are bags made?”

---

## Positioning B — Accessible luxury

**Promise:** An Italian handbag without the designer markup.

**Tests:** Does value framing improve conversion in €79–€129 vs pure craftsmanship?

**Risk:** “Luxury without markup” can sound dropshipping-like if visuals are weak.

**Evidence needed:** Price/value creative CTR and lead rate; qualitative replies.

---

## Positioning C — Timeless style

**Promise:** Timeless Italian design for everyday life.

**Tests:** Whether “timeless / classic” resonates more with 35–44 than trend language.

**Risk:** Vague without strong product design proof.

---

## Positioning D — Boutique / exclusivity

**Promise:** Small-batch Italian handbags, selected for Bulgaria.

**Tests:** Whether curation/scarcity-of-selection (honest: small catalog) lifts desire.

**Risk:** Must not invent false scarcity (“only 3 left”).

---

## Wave 1 testing map

| Channel | Variable |
| --- | --- |
| Ads | Rotate A, B, C hooks (and D lightly) |
| Landing page | Hold A+C hybrid constant |
| Later | A/B test headlines on page once traffic quality is proven |

---

## Claims policy

Every Italy / leather / construction claim on the page must map to **verified manufacturer facts**. Until verified, use placeholders clearly marked in content source files — never invent heritage stories.
